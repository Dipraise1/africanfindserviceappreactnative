Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how African Service Finder ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our App, including:

  • Personal Information: This includes your name, email address, phone number, and address.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect your precise location to show you nearby service providers.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our App, including your search history, service bookings, and favorites.
  • Device Information: Information about your mobile device, including device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
  • User Content: Information you provide when you create an account, book services, or leave reviews.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our App
  • Process and manage your service bookings
  • Connect you with service providers
  • Send you notifications about your bookings and account
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and customer service requests
  • Understand how users interact with our App to improve functionality
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

Sharing Your Information

We may share your personal information with:

  • Service Providers: We share your booking details with the service providers you choose to book.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: Companies that perform services on our behalf, such as payment processing, data analysis, email delivery, and hosting services.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.

Your Choices

You can control your personal information in the following ways:

  • Account Information: You can update your account information through the App's profile settings.
  • Location Data: You can enable or disable location services through your device settings.
  • Notifications: You can manage notification preferences in the App settings.
  • Data Access and Deletion: You can request access to your personal data or ask us to delete your account by contacting us.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

African Service Finder connects you with skilled professionals across Africa for all your service needs. Whether you're looking for plumbers, electricians, cleaners, carpenters, mechanics, or beauty services, our app makes it easy to find, book, and pay for services with just a few taps.

WHAT YOU CAN DO:

• Find Services Nearby: Discover service providers in your area with our location-based search
• Compare Providers: View detailed profiles with ratings, reviews, and pricing information
• Book Appointments: Schedule services for the date and time that works for you
• Secure Payments: Pay for services through our secure in-app payment system
• Track Bookings: Monitor the status of your current and past service appointments
• Rate & Review: Share your experience to help others find quality service providers
• Save Favorites: Keep a list of your preferred service providers for future needs
• Get Notifications: Receive updates about your bookings and special offers
• Manage Your Profile: Create and customize your customer or service provider profile
